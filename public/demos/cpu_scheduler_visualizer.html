FCFS — First Come, First Served

  • Processes are scheduled in arrival order.
  • Uses a simple FIFO queue — no decisions needed.
  • Non-preemptive: once started, runs to completion.
  • Suffers from the Convoy Effect — short jobs stuck behind long ones.
  • Formula: time = max(time, p.at) then time += p.bt
Advantages
  • Extremely simple to implement
  • No starvation (all processes eventually run)
  • Fair in terms of arrival order
Disadvantages
  • Convoy effect hurts average wait time
  • Not optimal for minimizing turnaround
  • Poor performance for interactive systems
